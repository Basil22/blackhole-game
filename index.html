⚫ Event Horizon

Real spaghettification. Throw objects into a black hole and watch tidal forces rip them apart — physics-accurate gravity, spring-mass materials, and a real event horizon.

How to play

  • 1. Pick an object (asteroid, astronaut, ship, planet)
  • 2. Tap THROW, then drag on screen to aim (arrow shows direction & power)
  • 3. Release to fling it toward the hole
  • 4. Tap SLOW-MO to watch the stretch and tears in slow motion
  • 5. Drag empty space to orbit the camera, pinch to zoom
  • 6. 📸 capture a frame, 📤 share it

The physics: every object is a network of spring-connected masses under real Newtonian gravity. The inner parts fall faster than the outer ones, stretching the object (spaghettification). When tidal stress beats a material's strength, it tears. At the event horizon r_s = 2GM/c², matter is consumed.
